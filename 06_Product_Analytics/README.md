# 📖 Overview

This phase focuses on transforming analytical findings into product strategy and business decisions.

While previous phases concentrated on data engineering, SQL analytics, machine learning, and business intelligence, this phase demonstrates how those technical insights can be translated into practical product improvements, feature prioritization, experimentation strategies, and business impact.

The objective is to showcase product thinking by connecting fraud analytics with measurable business outcomes and strategic product recommendations.
